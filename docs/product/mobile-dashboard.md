# Mobile dashboard

Public route: `/dashboard`. No sign-in, saved portfolio, AI call, or analytics is used on this display. The previous commerce subroutes stay redirected to the investment site.

- Reference-inspired indigo-to-coral background, translucent plum cards and prominent white clock/weather. Sunset is the default, independent of the main site’s appearance; the optional light appearance persists separately on this device. Styling is scoped to this display; the reference’s family photograph is not used. Reduced-transparency and increased-contrast settings use solid cards.
- Device-clock time and date in the chosen city's timezone. Dubai is the labelled default; city, temperature unit and clock format persist on that device.
- Open-Meteo current model weather, checked every 15 minutes while visible. Optional geolocation is rounded to two decimal places and sent directly to Open-Meteo only after the user requests it. Free endpoint is for this personal/non-commercial display; reassess its terms before a paid or advertising-supported launch.
- Native first-party quote cards replace the six TradingView embeds, which all failed on the user's Android Chrome device despite working in desktop/emulation. No TradingView frames, scripts, cookies, or sockets are needed by this dashboard.
- Sensex: TigZig's public daily `^BSESN` series (Yahoo Finance), dated daily close in index points. Nasdaq: XAUS's documented `nasdaq` 5-minute series (Yahoo Finance), latest available bar with UTC observation time, **not** a Nasdaq-100 CFD. Do not label either real-time. The former US 500 card has been replaced with Silver as requested.
- Gold (XAU), Silver (XAG), Bitcoin (BTC): Gold API's free, keyless `/price/{symbol}` endpoint, which explicitly permits website/commercial use. Indicative USD, metals per troy ounce; provider `updatedAt` is retained. No invented daily change percentage when it isn't supplied.
- AED → INR: Exchange Rate API's free AED-base endpoint; an end-use converter with the required attribution link, a dated daily reference rate and editable AED amount. It is not a bank/remittance quote or tick feed. Do not expose a general currency-data API. No fees or spreads included.
- The existing `/api/market-news?view=quotes` handler serves this fixed display universe to avoid another Vercel Hobby function. Independent bounded requests, concurrent request coalescing, 60-second CDN cache; provider cache is 60 seconds for spots, 2 minutes for Nasdaq, 15 minutes for daily Sensex and 1 hour for FX. Browser refresh is every minute while visible, also on reconnect/resume. No private holdings/account input enters these requests.
- A bad/missing quote never becomes zero. Validate price, expected symbol/currency, and observation date. Retain prior validated public quotes in memory/on-device during failures, explicitly label them Saved value/rate with original timestamp. Expire spot values after 24 hours, FX after 3 days, indices after 7 days. An earlier observation never replaces a newer one. No hardcoded market prices or hidden iframe fallback.
- Five public RSS queries grouped into India, UAE, USA and Fintech feeds, curated publishers, bounded requests and ten-minute caching. Headlines older than 72 hours or lacking a valid date/HTTPS link are excluded. Earlier results remain visibly dated during a source outage. Two headlines are visible at once, with balanced regional rotation every 15 seconds, country/Fintech filters and a pause control. Fintech follows the FinPeel Brief topic mix: payments, digital banking, funding, regulation, open finance, BNPL and AI banking, with worldwide and MENA/India queries. Specialist publishers (including Finextra, The Paypers and FinTech Futures) are allowed only for this lane; existing date/link/anti-spam checks remain. Ethereum has been removed; its space holds the currency converter and the larger news panel.
- User-activated fullscreen and Screen Wake Lock where supported; standalone home-screen manifest for iPhone/Android. No service-worker cache of market data: network connectivity is required. Browser/OS may release the wake lock; the control reflects the current state and reacquires when visible.

Sources: [Gold API docs](https://gold-api.com/docs), [Gold API permitted use](https://gold-api.com/faqs), [XAUS API](https://xaus.com/api/), [XAUS sources](https://xaus.com/about/), [TigZig daily series](https://www.tigzig.com/apis/tremor), [Exchange Rate API free endpoint](https://www.exchangerate-api.com/docs/free), [Open-Meteo terms](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms), [Screen Wake Lock](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Screen_Wake_Lock_API).

Verification: unit checks cover quote identity/currency/date validation, provider failure isolation/coalescing/cache expiry, original timestamps on outage, no backwards replacement, FX base direction, timezone boundaries and weather/news validity. Browser checks block every TradingView domain while checking native prices at 390×844, 844×390 and 1440×1000; also exercise AED input, fullscreen, wake lock, saved settings, offline/reconnect, total feed outage, last-known values on reload, retry recovery and manifest. Real providers must also be checked after deployment. No private holdings are changed.
