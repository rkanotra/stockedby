Here's a result we see every day: a customer asks ChatGPT for the best vitamin C serum in India. The AI recommends a well-known D2C brand — *first position, glowing description*. Then the customer asks: "where should I buy it?"

And the AI says: **Flipkart.**

Not the brand's own website — where the brand keeps 100% of the sale. Flipkart, where the brand pays 15–30% commission, competes with lookalikes on the same page, and never learns the customer's name.

The brand won the recommendation and lost the checkout. We call this the **checkout battle**, and most brand owners don't know it's happening.

## Why AI sends buyers to marketplaces

AI apps don't recommend from memory — they search the web and read what they find. And what they find is dominated by marketplaces:

**Marketplaces own the search results for your own products.** Search "buy [your brand] [your product]" on Google. For most Indian and Gulf brands, the first results are Flipkart, Amazon, Noon or Nykaa listings — not the brand's site. The AI reads the same results, so it links what ranks.

**Marketplace pages are easier for AI to read.** Amazon's product pages have structured prices, stock status, reviews and delivery info in formats AI understands instantly. Many brand websites are beautiful to humans and unreadable to machines.

**Marketplaces feel "safe" to recommend.** When the AI isn't sure your site is trustworthy or even functional, it defaults to the marketplace everyone knows.

## What it costs you

Take a brand doing ₹10 lakh/month, where AI-influenced buyers are becoming 10–20% of demand. If AI routes those buyers to marketplaces:

- **15–30% commission** on every routed sale
- **No customer data** — no email, no phone, no repeat-purchase relationship
- **Lookalike exposure** — marketplace pages show "similar products" from your competitors right under your listing
- **Price wars** — resellers and discounters on the same listing train customers to wait for deals

The cruelest part: the AI chose *you*. The recommendation was won. Only the money went elsewhere.

## How to win the checkout back

1. **Make your product pages machine-readable.** Clear product names, prices, stock status in structured formats. If AI can read your page as easily as Amazon's, it has a reason to link you. ([Check if AI can read your site →](https://stockedby.com/audit))
2. **Own your brand searches.** Your website should be the top result for "[your brand] + product". If a marketplace outranks you for your own name, the AI follows.
3. **Give AI a reason to prefer direct.** Exclusive bundles, first-party pricing, fast delivery info stated clearly on your site — AI apps increasingly mention *why* they chose a link.
4. **Recheck monthly.** Routing changes as AI apps update. A fix that works today needs confirming next month.

Every commission you pay on an AI-won sale is optional. The brands acting on this now are keeping margins the rest are giving away.
