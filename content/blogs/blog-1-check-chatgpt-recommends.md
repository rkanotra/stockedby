Your customers have changed how they shop. Before buying a face serum, earbuds, or a gift, millions now ask ChatGPT, Gemini or another AI app one simple question: *"What should I buy?"*

The AI answers with 4–5 brand names. If your brand is in that answer, you get the customer. If it isn't, your competitor does — and you never even know the sale happened.

So the most important question for any brand owner in 2026 is simple: **when a customer asks AI about your category, does your name come up?**

Here is how to find out.

## The manual way (free, 15 minutes)

Open ChatGPT and ask the way your customer would ask. Not "tell me about my brand" — real shopper questions:

- "best vitamin C serum under ₹700 for oily skin"
- "same day flower delivery in Dubai under AED 200"
- "سماعات بلوتوث عزل ضوضاء بسعر معقول" (if your customers ask in Arabic, test in Arabic)

Then look at three things:

1. **Is your brand in the answer?** At what position — first or fifth?
2. **Where does the AI send the buyer?** Your website, or Amazon and Flipkart? If it names a marketplace, you pay commission on a sale the AI won for you.
3. **Repeat on Gemini.** AI apps disagree with each other more than you'd expect. A brand can be #1 on ChatGPT and completely missing on Gemini.

Do this for 4–5 different questions, and write down what you see.

## The problems with the manual way

Three things make manual checking unreliable:

**Your account biases the answer.** ChatGPT remembers your history. If you've searched your own brand before, it may mention you more than it would for a stranger. Your customer's ChatGPT doesn't know your brand exists.

**One question isn't enough.** Shoppers ask in many ways — by budget, by problem ("my hair keeps falling"), by occasion ("Diwali gift under ₹1,500"), in Hinglish, in Arabic. Your brand might win one phrasing and lose the other five.

**You can't see the "why."** The AI reads specific websites before answering — review sites, comparison pages, marketplaces. Which sites it trusts decides who gets recommended. Manually, you can't see this list.
