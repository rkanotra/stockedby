Something quietly changed in how people shop in our region — and most brands haven't noticed yet.

Walk through what a young professional in Mumbai, Dubai or Riyadh does before buying today. They don't open ten tabs and compare. They open ChatGPT or Gemini and ask, in their own words:

- *"₹700 ke andar dull skin ke liye kaunsa vitamin C serum lena sahi rahega?"*
- *"توصيل ورد نفس اليوم بدبي لمناسبة الليلة"*
- *"best thobe fabric that stays crisp through a Riyadh summer workday"*

The AI answers with 4–5 specific brands. The shopper picks one. The entire "research phase" — where brands used to fight for attention with ads, SEO and influencers — collapsed into a single AI answer.

## Why this matters more here than in the US

Three reasons our region is ground zero for this shift:

**1. Mobile-first shoppers adopt AI assistants faster.** India is one of ChatGPT's largest user bases globally, and Gemini ships built into every Android phone — which is most phones in India. In the Gulf, AI adoption among young shoppers is among the highest in the world.

**2. Our languages are invisible to US tools.** Shoppers here ask in Hinglish and Arabic. The AI's answers in those languages come from different sources than English answers — meaning a brand can rank well in English tests and be completely absent when a real customer asks in Hinglish.

**3. Our marketplaces are different.** In the US it's Amazon. Here it's Flipkart, Meesho and Nykaa in India; Noon and Amazon.ae in the UAE; Amazon.sa, Jarir and Nice One in Saudi. When AI recommends and routes buyers, these are the names taking the checkout — and the commission.

## The three questions every brand owner should answer this month

**1. Does AI recommend you?** Ask the way your customers ask — in their language, with their budgets. Or [check free in 2 minutes](https://stockedby.com).

**2. Where does AI send your buyers?** If the answer is "Flipkart" or "Noon," you're paying commission on sales AI won for you.

**3. Can AI read your website?** If your product names, prices and stock aren't machine-readable, AI apps can't recommend or sell them — no matter how good your products are. [Check your site free](https://stockedby.com/audit).

## The window is open — briefly

Here's the honest opportunity: almost nobody in our region is working on AI visibility yet. The brands showing up in AI answers today are there mostly by accident — good reviews on the right sites, readable pages, luck.

That means a brand that works on this *deliberately* — even a small one — can outrank giants in AI answers within months. That window closes as the big players wake up. In the US, they already have.
